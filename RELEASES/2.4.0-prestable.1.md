## Prestable meaning

This version is considered suitable for daily real-world testing and rollback-safe deployment, but it is not yet marked stable. Promotion to stable should follow sustained workstation use without injector, hotkey, focus, or restart regressions.

## Promotion criteria

Promote this snapshot or a direct descendant to stable after a representative work period with:
- repeated RU/EN input and layout changes;
- 100+ normal expansions;
- repeated `Ctrl+Alt+M` / fallback use;
- form open/close cycles;
- worker/config restarts;
- no lost focus, duplicated text, stuck modifiers, or missed hotkeys.
